# Field Approve

Build a super simple, clean, and inviting field change-order protection app for construction subcontractors.

Key features and workflow:
- Fast mobile-first field capture: Foremen can select a job/project, snap or attach photos of the condition, enter a quick scope description, and type a flat lump-sum dollar figure.
- Flexible sign-off:
  1. On-screen signature: Hand the device directly to the GC superintendent to review scope and amount and draw their signature on screen.
  2. Remote SMS/link approval: Generate a shareable link (or SMS review screen) so the superintendent can review the photos, description, and price on their own phone and approve with a digital signature.
- Status tracking & dashboard: Track all change directives by job, status (Draft, Pending Approval, Approved, Rejected), date, and dollar amount.
- Signed backup summary: Generate a clean, professional change directive packet with timestamps, photos, dollar total, and signatures ready to export or email for the monthly pay application.
